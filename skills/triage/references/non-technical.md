# Tickets from non-technical people

A PM, a client or support describes what they saw or what they want, in the product's words. The ticket is right about the experience and often wrong about the cause.

- **Separate the sighting from the theory.** "The export is broken" may mean slow, empty, wrong or confusing. Find what they did, what they expected, and what happened instead.
- **Find the goal behind the ask.** A requested change ("add a button") may hide a problem that a smaller change solves. Name the problem first.
- **Reproduce their path:** their role, data, environment, browser and device. Most "cannot reproduce" is a different path.
- **Misuse is a finding, not a dismissal.** If the product allowed the mistake, the fix may be a guard, clearer wording or docs.
- **Ask in their words.** Few questions, each answerable without code: "Which page?", "What did you expect to see?", "Can you send a screenshot or the time it happened?". Offer choices when you can.
- **Answer in plain language:** what happens, why, and what changes. No code, no internal names. A bug's [Gherkin scenario](../../gherkin/SKILL.md) is the acceptance they can read and confirm.
